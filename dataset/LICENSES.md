## Ecuador MAG plantation inventory

Source: Ministerio de Agricultura y Ganadería (MAG), Subsecretaría de Producción Forestal (SPF), Ecuador. *Mapa del Inventario de Plantaciones Forestales Comerciales del Ecuador Continental, escala 1:5.000, año 2025*, product `mFC001_ec5k25_001` / `FC001_PLANTACION_FORESTAL_2025`.

The dataset-level resource constraints in the [official ISO metadata](http://geoportal.agricultura.gob.ec/geonetwork/srv/spa/xml.metadata.get?uuid=465c29e2-0692-4f88-939c-b45a1773ad72) explicitly specify **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: attribution, noncommercial use and share alike. See the [licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) and [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode). The record identifies the source download as `shp_plantacion_forestal_2025.zip`.

The release selects BALSA / VALIDADO stands, exports boundaries to GeoPackage, retains only selected attributes and adds research identifiers. Credit MAG/SPF, identify these changes and retain the source licence when redistributing the inventory or protected adaptations. Neither MIT nor CC BY 4.0 replaces these conditions. The research is not endorsed by MAG. Owner names and personal contact details are excluded.

The official metadata was dated 29 August 2025. Its downloaded XML SHA-256 is `4b9a7e9a0a1afbadefcbec17de2683cf44cd04ef4cc5fd9bbd7a79830bbd6d47`.

## Sentinel-1 numerical trajectories

**Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2016–2026).** Monthly numerical trajectories were organized using S1-GRiTS from NASA/JPL/OPERA RTC-S1 products distributed by ASF DAAC; source product DOI: [10.5067/SNWG/OPERA_L2_RTC-S1_V1](https://doi.org/10.5067/SNWG/OPERA_L2_RTC-S1_V1).

The [NASA/ASF product metadata](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/concepts/C2777436413-ASF.iso19115) marks the data as free and open and links the NASA data-use policy. The [Copernicus Sentinel notice](https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel) permits lawful reproduction, distribution, adaptation and combination, with source acknowledgement. Preserve those notices when reusing the numerical derivatives. The release does not include original bursts or DEM tiles.
