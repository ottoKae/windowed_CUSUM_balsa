\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Windowed CuSum reconstructs recurrent plantation harvests from decadal Sentinel-1 observations by renewing local baselines and aggregating pixelwise detections into stand-level histories. Across 14 benchmark configurations, it achieved the highest F1 ($0.837$) and recovered 83.9\% of adjacent reference harvest pairs, compared with 65.6\% for the strongest alternative. Similar dating accuracy on shared events indicates that the main improvement was the completeness of reconstructed histories.

Across mainland Ecuador, the framework identified 6,303 harvest events during 2017--2025. Repeated harvesting occurred in 59.6\% of evaluable stands, with a median inter-harvest interval of 34 months. The resulting histories captured spatial concentrations and temporal variation in harvesting while retaining intra-event spatial progression. Decadal SAR archives thus provide a basis for retrospective monitoring of plantation turnover in persistently cloudy regions, subject to observation coverage and the distinction between detected canopy loss and confirmed harvest.
